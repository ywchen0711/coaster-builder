# Coaster Builder 雲霄飛車設計師

Build a roller coaster piece by piece in 3D, then ride it. (three.js, no build step)

**Play:** https://ywchen0711.github.io/coaster-builder/

- **Pieces:** straight, pitch up / down (gentle and steep slopes), tight and wide turns, vertical loop, booster, brakes, and a chain lift for climbing. Track that would hit other track, the ground or leave the park is refused, and buttons that do not fit are greyed out. **Auto-finish** searches for a way back to the station.
- **Physics:** the train runs on energy (gravity, drag and rolling friction). Turns bank themselves to match the speed. Without a chain lift a train can stall on a hill, and the game says where.
- **Ratings:** excitement, intensity and nausea, plus top speed, drops, inversions, airtime and G-forces.
- **Super users:** Josh Chen (speed), Jasper Chen (loops), Jeff Chen (airtime) and Erin Lai (long, smooth rides) sit in the front cars. They shout during the ride and each gives stars and a comment afterwards. Their challenges, and a few more, earn badges.
- Four example coasters, a save slot, front-seat / chase / free views, Chinese and English (the lobby's `?lang=` is honoured), mouse or touch.

## Code
- `js/track.js` – pieces on a 4 m grid, collision checks, auto-complete (weighted A*)
- `js/sim.js` – path resampling, track frames, the ride, ratings, the super users' reviews, badges
- `js/main.js` – three.js rendering and UI · `js/i18n.js` – text · `js/presets.js` – generated by `tools/gen-presets.mjs`
- `node tools/test.mjs` – layout, collision, auto-complete and physics tests · `node tools/debug.mjs "S U* S* ..."` – ride a layout and print the forces
